Target classes

Six functional classes of CAZymes

1

Lignin-Modifying Oxidoreductases

Oxidative enzymes (e.g., laccases, peroxidases) capable of modifying or degrading lignin.

2

Cell Wall Esterases

Enzymes that remove acetyl and methyl groups from wall polymers, improving enzyme access.

3

Cellulases

Hydrolytic enzymes active against cellulose, the main structural polysaccharide of the plant cell wall.

4

Xylanases / Hemicellulases

Enzymes degrading xylan and other hemicelluloses, opening up the cell wall matrix.

5

Pectinases

Enzymes that modify or cleave pectin, contributing to cell wall loosening.

6

Other Glycoside Hydrolases

Diverse GH families acting on various carbohydrate substrates in the apoplast.
